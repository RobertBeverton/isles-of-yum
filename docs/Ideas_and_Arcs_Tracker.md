### Arc E: The Sweet Chain — full scaffold-development history (used in Story #10)

Kept here as a worked example of the concept-to-outline process for a future long-arc idea, since this one went through an unusually thorough multi-pass development before drafting.

Felix wants sweets, the shop's out of stock, and a trail of clues leads him and Alex back through the whole sugar-to-sweets chain (real-world grounding: cane harvested and crushed for juice → juice boiled/purified into raw sugar → sugar melted into syrup → syrup cooled/pulled/shaped/flavoured/coloured → wrapped finished sweets). Deliberate pacing choice: each stop gets real dwell-time (tasting, texture-play, sensory exploration), not just clue-and-move-on — the journey is the pleasure, not only the mystery. Beat count per island is variable by content, not fixed — earlier stops ran 2 beats (wonder/tasting, then clue-and-reveal), Part 3 earned more since it carried the emotional climax. Never narrate the chain in industrial "primary/secondary/tertiary" terms on the page; give each island its own vivid sensory identity first. Cast: Felix and Alex only.

- **Branching stakes:** sugar/syrup isn't only for sweets — as the boys trace the chain backward, each part reveals exactly ONE additional named destination also waiting on this supply, using already-established islands (no new world-building needed): Part 1 revealed syrup also goes to **Biscuit Town** (icing/glazes); Part 2 revealed raw sugar also goes to **Strawberry Hill** (its established jam operation). Each reveal happens in-scene via a character already present (not narration), stays a one-line affected-party rather than a new subplot to solve, and culminates in Part 3 with the grower himself realizing the true scope of who's been waiting on him — this folds the branching-stakes idea directly into his own "hoarding out of insecurity" emotional arc (his small private worry rippled further than he knew) rather than diluting it.
- **Part 1 — the Shop & the Shaping/Flavouring Island (Ribbon Cove).** Felix's want, the missing delivery, first rumour of a problem upstream. Richest tasting/texture stop (pulling, twisting, colouring, stamping). Ends discovering *they're* missing their syrup delivery, plus the Biscuit Town reveal — cliffhanger: problem is further upstream.
- **Part 2 — the Boiling/Syrup Island (Simmer Slopes).** Golden bubbling vats, texture/colour-change exploration (distinct from Toffee Apple Orchard's vat — refining/boiling, not dipping). Ends discovering they never received raw sugar, plus the Strawberry Hill reveal — cliffhanger: trail leads to the source.
- **Part 3 — the Source (Canebrake Grove, growing grove + crushing mill, one connected location).** Root cause: an old, quietly-anxious grower (Saffron) has been delaying harvest, afraid that once his apprentice (Clove) can judge ripeness too, nobody will need him anymore — a craft-rule-1a "hoarding out of insecurity" mischief-maker, resolved via conversation not punishment. Closes with delivery restarting, the Biscuit Town/Strawberry Hill coda, and a callback payoff at the shop.
- **Jeopardy beat:** the arc as scaffolded had zero physical jeopardy across Parts 1-2, an outlier against this world's pattern (rule 2). Decided, after comparing two proposed versions against rule 2 and this world's tonal precedent: the ground near the crushing mill is warned to be sticky with spilled syrup; the boys race across it; one loses a shoe to the stickiness and it gets pulled into the big rollers, coming out completely flattened — nobody hurt, a visible "that's what could've happened" consequence. Rejected alternative: a version where a person actually gets rolled flat by the rollers and is reinflated by a running gag "un-flat-person pump," treated as a casual yearly occurrence — persona review unanimously rejected this as (a) not actually satisfying rule 2's jeopardy definition since it explicitly has no fear/teamwork/relief-after-danger beat, and (b) a genuine tone break from this world's precedent, which has never treated physical harm to a person, even reversible/comedic, as routine.
- **Shoe-thread follow-on:** after the flattened shoe, the boys privately worry (their own anxious assumption, not a stated fact) that Mum won't let them go off alone again if a ruined shoe comes home. Resolution: the grower, now grateful, reshapes a bit of his own hardened sugar-work into a rough replacement shoe as a small thank-you gesture. Values-consistency check: the boys must NOT succeed via concealment (no precedent in this world for kids actively hiding something from parents) — landed instead as "turns out we didn't need to hide it."
- **Naming notes:** the grower's name (Saffron) was directed to evoke sensory expertise/knowing, gentle and formal-register like Marzipan, not jaunty/occupation-derived like Toffit. Two names had to be renamed after world-consistency-check caught collisions: "Ribbon Reef" (suffix collision with Fish Finger Reef) became Ribbon Cove; "Taffy" (too close in sound to Toffit) became Stripe.
- Persona-reviewed across five separate passes before and during drafting (concept pass, initial scaffold pass, a "how do we make this better" pass that added variable beat counts and branching stakes, a framework-completeness pass that added the jeopardy beat and shoe-thread, and full-draft persona-review on each finished part) — see Story Archive entry #10 for the finished craft takeaways.
